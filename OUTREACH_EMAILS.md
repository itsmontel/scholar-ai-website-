## Before you send anything

1. **Pricing is accurate as written.** Since the trial gate went live, new users
   need a card to reach the essay analyzer. Do not tell anyone the product is
   "free, no card required" because they will try it, hit the trial wall, and
   stop trusting you. Every email below offers a comped Pro account instead,
   which is normal practice and costs you nothing.
2. **Send from your real address**, not a noreply or a marketing tool. These
   should look like one person writing to another person.
3. **One follow up only.** Template at the bottom. Send it 7 days later. If
   there is no reply after that, move on.
4. **Send Monday to Thursday morning.** Friday pitches get buried.
5. **Track what you send** so you never double message the same site.

Hello,

I would like to pitch a guest post.

Working title: What Students Do With AI Feedback When Nobody Is Watching

The argument: the format of AI feedback changes whether students revise at
all. When a tool returns a holistic score, students read it as a verdict and
submit anyway. When it returns criterion-level feedback, for example "your

2. Holistic versus rubric scoring, including what the bias research shows

   teachers can hand to students directly
4. Ground rules worth setting so the feedback supports revision instead of
   replacing it

Around 950 words.

Context on me: I run WriteScholar, an essay feedback tool used by students, so
I see this behaviour in aggregate every day. The piece would be vendor
neutral, with no mention of my product in the body. Mention it in my bio or
leave it out entirely, whichever fits your policy.
