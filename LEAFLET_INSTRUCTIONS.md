## How to Use These Leaflets

### Printing Instructions:
1. Open any HTML file in your web browser
2. Press Ctrl/Cmd + P to print
3. Set paper size to Letter (8.5" x 11")
4. Choose "More settings" → "Print backgrounds" for full color
5. Print as many copies as needed

### Customization Options:
- **QR Code**: Replace the "QR Code Here" placeholder in Design 1 with an actual QR code linking to writescholar.com
- **Statistics**: Update the numbers (95%, 10k+) with your actual metrics
- **Testimonial**: Replace the sample testimonial in Design 2 with real student feedback
- **Colors**: Modify the CSS color schemes to match your brand colors

### Distribution Strategy:
- **Design 1**: Great for campus events, student centers, and coffee shops
- **Design 2**: Perfect for library bulletin boards and study areas
- **Design 3**: Ideal for academic departments, writing centers, and professor offices

### Digital Use:
These designs can also be converted to PDF format for:
- Email campaigns
- Social media posts (resize as needed)
- Digital bulletin boards
- Website landing page elements

## Tips for Maximum Impact:
1. **Target High-Traffic Areas**: Libraries, student unions, dining halls
2. **Timing**: Distribute before major assignment deadlines and exam periods
3. **Campus Partnerships**: Work with writing centers and tutoring services
4. **Student Organizations**: Partner with academic clubs and honor societies
