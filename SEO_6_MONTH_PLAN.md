## Month 3 — May 2026: Backlinks & Authority Building

**Goal:** Acquire 20–30 quality backlinks to raise domain authority.

### Link Building Tactics

**1. Free tool directories**
Submit your free tools to:
- toolpilot.ai
- futurepedia.io
- theresanaiforthat.com
- aitoolhunt.com
- topai.tools

Each submission = 1 backlink from a high-authority AI directory. These are easy wins.

**2. Student/education blogs — guest posting**
Reach out to student blogs or academic writing blogs and offer a guest post. Topics:
- "5 AI Tools That Actually Help With Studying (Without Cheating)"
- "How to Write a Thesis Statement in 5 Minutes"

**3. Broken link building**
Use a tool like Ahrefs (free trial) or Moz to find broken links on student resource sites, then pitch your content as a replacement.

**4. Social profiles (brand signals)**
Create and fill out profiles on:
- Twitter/X (@writescholar — already in your meta tags)
- LinkedIn (WriteScholar company page)
- Instagram (student audience is here)
- TikTok (short "study tips" videos linking to the site)

### Blog: 2 posts/week
Continue with Cluster 1, 2, 3 content from Month 2 plan.

## Month 4 — June 2026: Conversion & UX Signals

**Goal:** Improve user engagement metrics (dwell time, bounce rate) which help rankings.

### What to focus on

**Expand tool pages with more content**  
Google rewards pages where users stay longer. For each major tool page, add:
- A "How to use this tool" step-by-step section (3–5 steps)
- A "Who is this for?" section
- A comparison table vs. competitors (e.g. "WriteScholar Quiz Generator vs. Quizlet")
- More FAQ questions (aim for 5–8 per tool page)

**Add schema markup to tool pages**  
Your homepage already has FAQ and SoftwareApplication schema. Add `FAQPage` schema to each tool page — this enables rich snippets (expandable Q&As in search results) which increase click-through rate.

**Image SEO**  
- Add descriptive `alt` text to all images/SVGs on tool pages and blog posts
- Compress images if any are large (helps Core Web Vitals)

### Blog: 2 posts/week
Focus this month on **comparison posts** — these rank well and convert:
- "WriteScholar vs Quillbot: Which is Better for Students?"
- "WriteScholar vs Grammarly: What's the Difference?"
- "Best AI Tools for Students in 2026 (Compared)"
- "Free vs Paid: Which WriteScholar Plan is Right for You?"

## Month 6 — August 2026: Authority & Long-Term Flywheel

**Goal:** Establish WriteScholar as the go-to reference for student AI tools.

### Domain Authority Push
By Month 6, target 50+ backlinks total. Additional tactics:
- **HARO (Help a Reporter Out)** — respond to journalist queries about AI, education, or study tools. Getting quoted = high-authority backlink.
- **University student blogs** — reach out to university writing centres or student newspapers, offer a free writeup or tool demo
- **YouTube** — start a WriteScholar YouTube channel with short tutorial videos for each tool. YouTube videos often rank in Google and drive backlinks

### Content Calendar
By the end of Month 6, aim for **35–40 total blog posts**. With 9 already published, that means publishing roughly 2 posts/week across months 1–6.

### Review structured data
- Add `HowTo` schema to tutorial blog posts
- Add `BlogPosting` schema to all blog posts (title, date, author, description)
- Ensure `FAQPage` schema is on every tool page
